# Bristlecone Pine Seed 0.0.2 — Recovery Provenance

## Recovery conclusion

The extracted **Bristlecone Pine Newelle Seed 0.0.2** is a coherent historical bootstrap package and is retained here as Bristlecone's recovered Seed 0.0.2 identity baseline.

This conclusion is intentionally narrow:

> **Seed 0.0.2 is reconstructed as a logical historical artifact.**

It does **not** establish that these files were Bristlecone's final identity/configuration at the end of Qubes-era development. Later live Forest state, Soul changes, Grain changes, runtime policy, or Post-Seed identity material must be reconciled separately.

## Authority interpretation

These are surviving source artifacts from a packaged Seed, not merely continuity prose.

They establish what Seed 0.0.2 was intended to plant and preserve. They do not, by themselves, prove the final runtime state after later Phase 14 development.

The planting script is retained as **historical implementation evidence**. It must not be treated as the installer for the modern Post-Apollo Forest without review against the current Hermes/runtime environment and the planned Rust/QML architecture.
